## Company page logo

Use the [canonical monogram logo](../logo/png/grayhaven-logo-monogram.png) for
the LinkedIn company page profile image. LinkedIn's profile logo slot is small
and square, so the monogram remains legible where the full wordmark would be
reduced too far.

Use the monogram as provided. Do not crop the circular border or replace its
background treatment.

## Company page banner

Use [linkedin-banner.png](../assets/linkedin-banner.png) as the Grayhaven
Systems LLC company page banner. It uses a deep graphite, slate, and blue
palette with restrained infrastructure-inspired geometry. The banner does not
repeat the wordmark; the monogram serves as the page's compact identity mark.

Keep the banner free of additional text or overlays. LinkedIn may crop the
banner differently across page layouts and screen sizes, so the central quiet
area should remain unobstructed.
