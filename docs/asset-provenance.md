## Purpose

This document records the external sources and licenses for bundled third-party
assets. It applies to assets used in any Grayhaven Systems LLC branded output,
not only websites.

[Back to top](#asset-provenance)

## Bundled assets

### Inter

- Upstream: [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter)
- Version: 4.1, from the official
  [Inter 4.1 release](https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1)
- Files: `assets/fonts/inter-*.ttf`
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, as provided by the upstream project.

### Font Awesome Free

- Upstream: [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/)
- Version: 6.5.1
- Files: `style-guide/fontawesome.min.css` and `assets/webfonts/fa-*`
- Licenses: icons under CC BY 4.0, fonts under SIL OFL 1.1, and code under MIT.

## Adding or updating assets

Before adding a third-party asset, record its upstream name, exact version or
commit, source URL, local destination, and applicable license here. Prefer
local copies where practical and legally permitted. Do not add a CDN reference
without documenting why a local copy is unsuitable.
