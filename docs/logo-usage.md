# Logo Usage

[Return to README](../README.md)

Grayhaven Systems LLC provides a full wordmark logo in light and dark variants
and a universal monogram logo in SVG and PNG formats.

## Wordmark Usage

The wordmark is the preferred logo and is suitable for websites, business
cards, letterheads, documents, presentations, and other branded media.

Use [grayhaven-logo-wordmark-light.svg](../logo/svg/grayhaven-logo-wordmark-light.svg)
on light backgrounds.

Use [grayhaven-logo-wordmark-dark.svg](../logo/svg/grayhaven-logo-wordmark-dark.svg)
on dark graphite, gunmetal, or dark aluminum backgrounds.

The dark-background wordmark uses:

- Soft White wordmark text: `#E6EAF0`
- Brightened graphite mark gradient: `#5D6874` to `#3B4651`
- Divider/support marks: `#444C56`

Do not use the light-background wordmark directly on dark surfaces unless the
surface behind the logo is intentionally light.

## Monogram Usage

The monogram logo carries its own filled circular background and can be used on
light or dark surfaces. It is suitable for profile pictures, avatars, favicon
exploration, Discord profile images, Reddit profile images, and compact brand
contexts.

The monogram logo should be used as-is. Do not crop it into a square that cuts
off the circular border or mark.

## File Formats

SVG should be used where possible.

Use PNG for platforms that do not support SVG uploads. PNG files are rendered
with transparent backgrounds where appropriate:

- Wordmarks have transparent backgrounds.
- The monogram/profile asset has transparent corners and a filled circular
  background.
